Questions

What are your opening hours?

We're open every day from 8:00 in the morning until midnight. Breakfast is served from 8:00.

How do I book a table?

Message us on WhatsApp or call +971 52 365 2723 — tell us the date, time and number of guests.

What is in the All Day Brunch?

For AED 70: one hot main (plov, chicken cutlets, manti or beef stroganoff), one salad (vinaigrette, achichuk, Olivier or Greek), samsa, and a drink — kompot, Coca-Cola, Sprite, Fanta or tea. Served all day.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver across all of Dubai. Add your address when ordering — you can also share your location so the courier finds you faster.

Can I order online?

Yes — our full menu with photos is on our online ordering page. Tap “Order online” anywhere on this site.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes: syrniki, crêpes, porridges, omelet, shakshuka, salads like achichuk, shakarob and Greek salad, pumpkin samsa and more.

Where exactly are you?

Shop 6001, Greens Building 6, Sobha Hartland, Mohammed Bin Rashid City, Dubai.
